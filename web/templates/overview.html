Methodology, data sources & export

A transparent starting point

Enter your current Nordnet holdings and weighted average purchase price. Use zero shares for a watchlist company. Norwegian provider tickers end in .OL. For NOK holdings you can enter a bare Oslo ticker such as KOG; the suffix is added during lookup.

Refresh requests five years of daily price history and USD/NOK from an unofficial Yahoo Finance endpoint. Availability is not guaranteed. Prices may be delayed. Every quote shows its provider timestamp; quotes older than four calendar days are excluded from NOK valuation. USD holdings also require a fresh NOK exchange rate.

Alerts use your price levels, a 200-session price average, and your chosen position concentration limit, including manually tracked cash, when every holding is priced. These are review prompts, not validated investment recommendations. The Recommendations page adds technical ranking and background analysis. Explore also shows available annual financial figures, sources and dates. Official announcement metadata is shown separately from ranks; Source-linked annual assessments, transaction imports, cash reconciliation and optional open-tab desktop alerts are available. Full-report news interpretation, tax calculations and automatic trades are not implemented.

Keep shares and average cost updated after trades and stock splits. Review corporate actions with your broker. The research worker refreshes holdings and the candidate universe at the configured interval while the server is running.

Your records are stored in the Northstar server’s SQLite database. Export a backup below. This application does not connect to your Nordnet account.
