Your allocation settings

Enter cash available in the account you track here and one total budget shared across the buy plan. The fee limit defaults to 2% of stock value. A technical sale review defaults to 25% of a holding, rounded up; set it to 0 to disable that rule. Starting limits are illustrative settings, not recommended allocations. Update cash manually after real trades.

Purchase fee: 79 kr per order, regardless of share count, using your stated Nordnet fee. Budgets include this fee. Selling fees are not yet modelled.

Transactions, imports & cash reconciliation

Transaction history & cash reconciliation

1. Verify an opening snapshot

First import your current holdings CSV and verify available cash in Portfolio fit. Choose the date those holdings and cash represent, after all activity that day. Earlier and same-day transactions are history only; later new transactions change holdings and cash. Reinitializing keeps transaction history.

2. Import a transaction-history CSV

This is a different file from the holdings export. Map the columns once. Net cash must be signed and include actual broker charges; the estimated 79 kr purchase fee is never added again. Only NOK cash and Oslo stock transactions are supported. Transfers and unsupported events require reconciliation. A split needs a separate mapped split-ratio column (for example 2:1), zero net cash, and a confirmed ticker; quantities are never guessed from broker share deltas.
