Research settings & methodology

The default universe contains 180 Oslo tickers from a dated listing snapshot (26 October 2026). It is broad, but not a complete or automatically maintained exchange directory. Add up to 500 tickers; missing data and liquidity exclusions are shown. Snapshot source ↗

Overall score: Trend 30%, Momentum 25%, Risk 20%, Timing 15%, Liquidity 10%. Trend measures price relative to 50/200-session averages. Momentum combines 3/6-month returns scaled by volatility. Risk penalises historical volatility and maximum drawdown. Timing uses RSI, MACD and distance from MA50. Liquidity uses average daily turnover. Each component and its explanation is available in the explorer.

Top-20 ranking and buy signals share one entry policy: at least 200 recent daily observations, matching currency, all four trend conditions, the configured turnover floor with 15 valid observations, volatility below 50%, and daily close less than 15% above MA50. Component scores order qualifying entries. Other stocks retain diagnostic scores without entry ranks. These scores are not probabilities or validated forecasts.

Holdings: consider reducing when close is below its 200-session average and three-month momentum is negative. Your lower/upper price levels take priority for sell/profit reviews. Failing the entry rules alone does not trigger selling. Position, sector and reserve limits can add separate reductions in the share-count plan. No optimal execution date or future price is predicted. Historical series use adjusted close when the provider supplies it; charts label the basis.
