How these statistics are measured

Selected-window returns use the first and last displayed closes. Volatility uses the sample standard deviation of daily simple returns, annualised with 252 sessions. Historical 95% VaR is the interpolated 5th-percentile daily loss; expected shortfall averages the worst 5% probability mass. Both are sample descriptions, not maximum possible losses or forecasts. Risk ratios assume a zero risk-free rate. Comparison returns, correlation and beta use shared UTC dates; at least 20 shared-date return intervals are required for correlation and beta. Missing dates are not filled. Drawdown in the lower chart uses the peak of all loaded history; selected-period drawdown starts at the visible window.

Annual history & calculation notes

Revenue growth compares consecutive annual periods only. Margins and debt ratios require matching fiscal dates and known matching currencies. The P/E proxy uses the latest quote and annual diluted EPS; it is not a trailing-twelve-month ratio. Share-count changes can reflect splits or acquisitions, as well as new issuance. Debt ratios require sector context, especially for banks and insurers.
