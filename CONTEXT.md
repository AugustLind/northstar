# Northstar investment research

This context describes Northstar's stock research and the evidence used to support an investor's review.

## Language

**Forward return estimate**:
A summary of the range of historical outcomes for a stock over a stated future horizon. Its median and percentile range describe scenarios in the observed data; they do not promise a future return.

**Decision brief**:
A concise view of a stock's current research context, with links to the evidence behind it.
