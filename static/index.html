Research settings & methodology

The starter universe contains 34 names from the publicly visible Euronext small-cap list dated 30 September 2026. It is a historical subset, not the whole market or a claim of current index membership. Add any Oslo ticker; .OL is added automatically. Universe source ↗

Buy screen: the latest daily close is above both 50- and 200-session averages, the 50-session average is above the 200-session average, and 63-session momentum is positive. Annualised volatility must be below 50%, and price less than 15% above its 50-session average. Each of the four trend conditions contributes 25 points. Buy candidates also need at least 15 valid turnover observations over the latest 20 sessions and average traded value above your liquidity floor (default NOK 250,000 per day). Candidates are ranked by passing the buy screen, then trend score, then three-month momentum divided by volatility (with a 10% volatility floor). Market size is not a ranking input.

Holdings: consider reducing when close is below its 200-session average and three-month momentum is negative. Your lower/upper price levels take priority for sell/profit reviews. Concentration is shown as context, not an automatic sell instruction. No optimal execution date or future price is predicted. Historical series use adjusted close when the provider supplies it; charts label the basis.

The worker runs while the Python process is running, even with the browser closed. It does not run while the computer is asleep or off. Local AI requires Ollama plus a downloaded model. Enable it above after setup: ollama pull qwen2.5:3b. There are no paid API calls; hardware and electricity are yours.

A transparent starting point

Enter your current Nordnet holdings and weighted average purchase price. Use zero shares for a watchlist company. Norwegian provider tickers end in .OL. For NOK holdings you can enter a bare Oslo ticker such as KOG; the suffix is added during lookup.

Refresh requests one year of daily price history and USD/NOK from an unofficial Yahoo Finance endpoint. Availability is not guaranteed. Prices may be delayed. Every quote shows its provider timestamp; quotes older than four calendar days are excluded from NOK valuation. USD holdings also require a fresh NOK exchange rate.

Alerts use your price levels, a 200-session price average, and a 25% portfolio concentration threshold when every holding is priced. These are review prompts, not validated investment recommendations. The Recommendations page adds a price-based screen and background analysis. No fundamentals, ingested news, tax calculations, transaction ledger, automatic trades or external notifications are implemented.

Keep shares and average cost updated after trades and stock splits. Review corporate actions with your broker. The research worker refreshes holdings and the candidate universe at the configured interval while the server is running.

Your records are stored in the Northstar server’s SQLite database. Export a backup below. This application does not connect to your Nordnet account.
