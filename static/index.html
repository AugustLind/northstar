Research settings & methodology

The default universe contains 180 Oslo tickers from a dated listing snapshot (26 October 2026). It is broad, but not a complete or automatically maintained exchange directory. Add up to 500 tickers; missing data and liquidity exclusions are shown. Snapshot source ↗

Overall score: Trend 30%, Momentum 25%, Risk 20%, Timing 15%, Liquidity 10%. Trend measures price relative to 50/200-session averages. Momentum combines 3/6-month returns scaled by volatility. Risk penalises historical volatility and maximum drawdown. Timing uses RSI, MACD and distance from MA50. Liquidity uses average daily turnover. Each component and its explanation is available in the explorer.

Top-20 eligibility requires at least 200 recent daily observations, the configured turnover floor with 15 valid observations, and volatility below 80%. Eligible stocks can still carry a Wait / watch label. The stricter Consider buying signal requires all four trend conditions, volatility below 50%, and price less than 15% above MA50. Neither score is a probability or a backtested forecast.

Holdings: consider reducing when close is below its 200-session average and three-month momentum is negative. Your lower/upper price levels take priority for sell/profit reviews. Concentration is shown as context, not an automatic sell instruction. No optimal execution date or future price is predicted. Historical series use adjusted close when the provider supplies it; charts label the basis.

The worker runs while the Python process is running, even with the browser closed. It does not run while the computer is asleep or off. Local AI requires Ollama plus a downloaded model. Enable it above after setup: ollama pull qwen2.5:3b. There are no paid API calls; hardware and electricity are yours.

A transparent starting point

Enter your current Nordnet holdings and weighted average purchase price. Use zero shares for a watchlist company. Norwegian provider tickers end in .OL. For NOK holdings you can enter a bare Oslo ticker such as KOG; the suffix is added during lookup.

Refresh requests two years of daily price history and USD/NOK from an unofficial Yahoo Finance endpoint. Availability is not guaranteed. Prices may be delayed. Every quote shows its provider timestamp; quotes older than four calendar days are excluded from NOK valuation. USD holdings also require a fresh NOK exchange rate.

Alerts use your price levels, a 200-session price average, and your chosen position concentration limit, including manually tracked cash, when every holding is priced. These are review prompts, not validated investment recommendations. The Recommendations page adds technical ranking and background analysis. Explore also shows available annual financial figures, sources and dates. Official announcement metadata is shown separately from ranks; full-report news interpretation, tax calculations, a transaction ledger, automatic trades and external notifications are not implemented.

Keep shares and average cost updated after trades and stock splits. Review corporate actions with your broker. The research worker refreshes holdings and the candidate universe at the configured interval while the server is running.
